About Me

A Practical Path Into Technology

I do not view my transition from the Marine Corps into technology as starting over. It is a continuation of the same commitment to leadership, service, continuous learning, and building systems that people can depend on.

My professional journey began in the United States Marine Corps, where I served from 2017 to 2022 in security forces, infantry leadership, and combat marksmanship instruction. At a nuclear security facility, I operated under strict access-control, compliance, and incident-response procedures while supporting classified security software and mission-critical physical-security systems.

After completing my military service, I carried that same sense of purpose into Farmingdale State College, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Programming and Information Systems. College gave me a new mission: transforming my leadership and problem-solving experience into technical capability.

That journey led me to the Long Island Systems User Group, where I advanced from Software Development Intern to Board Member. At LISUG, I have helped turn speaker, sponsorship, event, and board requirements into working technology for an IBM i-focused professional community.
